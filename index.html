Our Heritage

A Palace Table,
Reimagined.

Falaknuma draws its name from the legendary palace above the clouds — Hyderabad's most revered symbol of Nizami grandeur. We bring the royal kitchens of the Nawabs to Bengaluru: recipes preserved across centuries, crafted with the patience and artistry that define authentic Hyderabadi cuisine.

The Philosophy

The Art
of Dum.

Dum — the ancient technique of slow-cooking within a sealed vessel — is not a method. It is a ritual. Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the patience of centuries, the warmth of the subcontinent, and the precision of a royal chef's hand.
